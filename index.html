What is Aegis Quant?+

Aegis Quant is a product vision for market intelligence, quantitative research, and execution simulation. This landing page explains the approach, and the linked dashboard demonstrates the experience with synthetic examples.

What do “quantitative” and “agentic” mean?+

Quantitative research uses data and repeatable rules to study markets. Agentic research uses specialized software agents to help investigate and explain ideas. In the intended design, agents propose; independent controls retain authority over risk and accounting.

Will this website place real trades?+

No. The current scope is research and simulation. The website contains no live order-submission adapters and cannot be switched into live trading.

Are the prices, results, and AI activity real?+

The examples are clearly labeled synthetic demonstrations. They explain the interface and workflow, not investment performance. Live market feeds, model training, and autonomous agents are not connected.

Which markets does the product design cover?+

Cryptocurrency, US equities, and Indian equities. Each has its own currencies, sources, sessions, and constraints. Comparisons preserve those differences rather than treating every market as interchangeable.

Does a positive price difference mean guaranteed profit?+

No. Fees, available liquidity, timing, inventory, permissions, and risk can outweigh a price difference. A positive modeled estimate is still uncertain. Synthetic examples are not evidence of real profitability.
